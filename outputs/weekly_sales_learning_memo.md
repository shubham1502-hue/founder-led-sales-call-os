- Timing: 4 calls. Response angle: Connect the system to the next board, pipeline, or founder review date.
- Integrations: 2 calls. Response angle: Start with CSV exports from the tools they already use before promising automation.
- Stakeholder Alignment: 2 calls. Response angle: Create stakeholder-specific recap notes and founder-to-founder follow-up paths.
- Lack Of Urgency: 1 calls. Response angle: Ask what will happen if the same objections repeat for another month.
- Switching Cost: 1 calls. Response angle: Position the workflow as an export and synthesis layer, not a CRM replacement.
