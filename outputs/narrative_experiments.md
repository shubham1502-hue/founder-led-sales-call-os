## Objections that repeated
- Price: 7 calls. Angle: Tie cost to recovered founder time, fewer slipped follow-ups, and rescued late-stage deals.
- Security: 4 calls. Angle: Explain offline operation, anonymized examples, and data-minimizing usage.
- Timing: 4 calls. Angle: Connect the system to the next board, pipeline, or founder review date.
- Integrations: 2 calls. Angle: Start with CSV exports from the tools they already use before promising automation.
- Stakeholder Alignment: 2 calls. Angle: Create stakeholder-specific recap notes and founder-to-founder follow-up paths.

## Messaging angles to test

- Test an offline and anonymized-data angle for sensitive sales notes.
- Test a no-migration angle: export notes from the tools you already use.
- Test a category line: post-call intelligence for founder-led sales, not another CRM.
- Test proof-led messaging with sample memos, objection banks, and deal rescue queues.

- Who owns the follow-up risk after a sales call today?
- Which deal would this have helped rescue last month?
- What proof would make this feel like a business priority instead of a workflow improvement?
