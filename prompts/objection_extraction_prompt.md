# Objection Extraction Prompt

Use this prompt when you want to manually review sales call notes with an AI assistant.

## Prompt

You are helping an early-stage founder learn from founder-led sales calls.

Analyze the call notes below. Extract only what the prospect actually said or clearly implied. Do not invent details.

Return the answer in this structure:

1. Repeated or likely objections
2. Exact phrases from the notes that support each objection
3. Objection theme
4. Severity: high, medium, or low
5. Suggested founder response angle
6. Product or narrative implication
7. Proof point needed

Objection themes to consider:

- Price
- Timing
- Switching cost
- Internal bandwidth
- Security
- Integrations
- Unclear ROI
- Lack of urgency
- Stakeholder alignment
- Competitor comparison

PASTE CALL NOTES HERE
